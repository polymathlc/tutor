🔑 Does it have an answer key?

Chung GPT reads the first and last pages of the paper when it is uploaded: it fills in the level, the subject and the name if you left them blank, and if the answers are printed at the back it finds those pages and puts them away — you will not see them, and they are not marked. Attach a separate marking scheme here if you have one; it is never shown on screen, only used to mark you and to aim the hints.

🔑 The answer key

An answer key is for Chung GPT, not for you. Any page ticked here is taken out of your worksheet — you will not see it, it is not marked, and it never turns up in your mistake book — and it is read once so Chung GPT can mark against it and aim its hints. How much of a hint you are told is still decided by the help level, exactly as before.
