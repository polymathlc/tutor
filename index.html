🔑 The answer key

An answer key is for your buddy, not for you. Any page ticked here is taken out of your worksheet — you will not see it, it is not marked, and it never turns up in your mistake book — and it is read once so your buddy can mark against it and aim its hints. How much of a hint you are told is still decided by the help level, exactly as before.
